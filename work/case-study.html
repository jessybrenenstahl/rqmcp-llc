When “the run ended” became “the lesson helped.”

An administrative stop was being counted as evidence about unfinished work. That could award helpful credit to a lesson without the relevant outcome.

The starting failure

A workflow recalled a lesson and followed its planning instruction. A smoke-check child failed. Another child, still carrying out a repair, was stopped when the parent run ended. Its relevant work outcome was unknown.

The settlement reader could treat the stopped or cancelled child as evidence that the original obstacle had not recurred, then emit a helpful-feedback intent.

The bounded correction

Keep lifecycle settlement separate from the observed work outcome. With no relevant outcome, recurrence remains UNKNOWN and helpful credit cannot be awarded. An actual diagnostic remains evidence; normal completed work retains its bounded feedback behavior.
